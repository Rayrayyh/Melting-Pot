# Streak research and directions

Researched 2026-09-02 for the owner's request to fix the contribution streak. Four researchers spent at least twenty minutes each across the App Store listings, Mobbin and Dribbble captures, Awwwards winners and the open web, reading about sixteen products apiece, and a lead critic measured the current modal against what they found. The reference captures live outside the repo; the file names below identify them.

The rule this is measured against: the streak is a private record of one person's own days, never compared to anyone, and a quiet stretch shows the run they already managed rather than a zero.

## What the market does

- Not one of the sixteen products fires its streak celebration on app open; Duolingo's is the post-lesson screen, Finch's is a milestone moment, and everyone else (Peloton, Streaks, Apple, GitHub, Anki) has no celebration at all, just an ambient number or a calendar.
- The everyday streak surface is one line in the home header (Peloton '3 days Streak' plus seven dots; Streaks' count inside a ring), while the big-number screen is reserved for the moment right after the qualifying action or a milestone.
- The seven-day strip is universal and always distinguishes three states: past days done (filled check), today (outlined or colored label), and future days (dim), with Sunday-or-Monday start matching the product's week rule.
- Longer history is always a calendar or heatmap (GitHub 53x7 squares, Anki blue squares, Peloton month grid, Streaks dot grid) where a gap is just an empty cell, so a broken run never erases what came before.
- 'Best' or 'longest' streak is stored and shown as a permanent figure separate from the current run (Streaks '21 BEST STREAK', Apple's 'Longest Move Streak' award, Peloton's daily versus weekly), which is exactly how a quiet stretch can still show the run already managed.
- Grace is the norm now, not the exception: Duolingo freezes equip in advance and apply automatically, Brilliant streak charges are earned by doing the work and auto-apply (max two), Finch repairs retroactively, Strava and Todoist restore when the activity is uploaded or reported late, Streaks and Todoist let you declare days that do not count.
- Weekly granularity is common for the durable run (Strava's whole streak is weekly, Peloton keeps a weekly streak beside the daily one, Apple's Perfect Week), which fits a student's real rhythm better than a daily count.
- When products compare, they compare you to your own earlier period (Peloton '2 active days vs. previous month', Strava '12% more than the previous time period', Streaks '80.1% all time'), never in the streak UI to another person.
- Copy on the celebration is one short second-person sentence plus a number and a unit ('3 day streak', '30 DAY STREAK', 'You kept going for a whole month!'), with a single primary button.
- Reminders are opt-in rows in settings (Duolingo 'Daily practice and streak reminders', Streaks 'No reminders / Automatic / Custom'), not modals.
- Habitica, Headspace, Kahoot, Brilliant, Quizlet, Todoist, Strava and Snapchat all keep streaks out of their App Store screenshots even though the features exist; the streak is treated as a supporting mechanic, not a headline.
- The premium habit apps keep the streak count inside the control you act on (a ring with a number) and have no separate celebration surface at all.
- Where a celebration exists it is a state change of the thing just touched: a ring closing, a disc going solid, a toast-sized card at the end of the session; none of the restrained examples open a modal over a different page.
- Best streak, all-time completion rate and total completions are the headline statistics; the current run is secondary, which is what lets a broken run read as history rather than failure.
- The only comparison in the restrained products is against the person's own previous month, stated in one grey line.
- Calendars are dot grids or ringed dates; today is an open ring, done days are filled, missed days are blank or a tiny neutral mark, never red and never captioned.
- Award-winning stat reveals move slowly: numeral fades in, caption follows, one detail lights up; no particles, no bounce, no sound.
- Giant numeral plus small caption plus hairline divider is the shared typographic vocabulary for milestones and timelines across the Awwwards entries.
- Gentle products write in explanatory sentences ('This workout has likely boosted your mood'); gamified ones use exclamation marks, emoji and 'you're almost there' nudges.
- Pause and activity-status controls (Tappsk Pause, Gentler Streak activity status) let a person step away without losing the record, and the copy says so plainly.
- Wellness brands that win Awwwards (Health By Habit, Oura, HEVA, Glean) show no tracking UI on their marketing sites; the usable streak references come from Apple Design Award apps and from Awwwards entries that are themselves campaign trackers, so App Store screenshots were the reachable source for real screens.
- Reachability note: Awwwards was fully reachable by curl with raw-HTML parsing (thumbnails are lazy-loaded so the markdown fetch loses them); godly.website now redirects to recent.design with no habit matches; land-book and lapa.ninja sit behind a Cloudflare challenge; siteinspire returned an empty shell; Mobbin was not attempted on this beat.
- Every shipped celebration in the set (Duolingo, Brilliant, Nike Training Club, Superhuman, Kindle, Zero, Yousician) fires at the end of the action's own flow with a Continue or Close, never on opening the app; the streak on the home screen is an inline row (Peloton) or a card, not a modal.
- The universal counter is a big numeral with a unit label under it ('3 / day streak', '1 fast', '21 / Days in a row'); when two numbers appear they are 'Current' and 'Previous record' or 'Best' (Drops, Streaks), which maps onto MeltingPot's current and longest.
- Week strips are weekday initials with filled circles or ticks for done days and today outlined or tinted (Duolingo, Peloton, Done, Babbel); Duolingo anchors the strip to the calendar week in marketing but starts it from today in the post-lesson screen.
- Month calendars mark only the days that happened and outline today (Drops, Babbel, Paired, Peloton, Kindle); the honest ones leave future days blank, the pushy ones draw grey placeholders on every future day (Babbel).
- Containers split three ways: full-screen interstitial inside a flow (Duolingo, Brilliant, Nike), centred modal over the page triggered by the accomplishment (Zero, Superhuman, Audible), and bottom sheet for the record itself (Atoms, Drops); toasts are rare (Superhuman's footer line) and none of the products use a toast for the daily acknowledgement.
- Achievement pages are dominated by ladders of greyed unearned badges (Reddit, Zero '1/9', Atoms, Aaptiv, Skillshare), which is the single most common pattern and the one MeltingPot's rules push against.
- Copy is almost always exclamatory and imperative ('Keep it up!', 'Don't let up!', 'keep your daily streak'); the calmest voices are descriptive and dated (Zero '1 fast, 24 Mar 2019', Kindle 'Days in a row', Skillshare 'only visible to you').
- Freeze, rescue and charge mechanics (Paired, Drops, Brilliant) appear wherever a run can die; a rule that a quiet stretch keeps the longest run makes them unnecessary.
- Sharing is the default exit of most streak screens (Duolingo, Drops, Streaks, Nike, Zero, Superhuman); the private ones (Skillshare, Audible) say so in a footnote, which is what MeltingPot already does with 'Only you see this'.
- Reachability notes: Mobbin's search and flow pages are login-gated (the 'streak' search returns a not-found shell with a random quiz widget), but its SEO gallery pages (/explore/mobile/screens/achievements-awards, /explore/mobile/screens/progress, /explore/web/screens/achievements-awards, /explore/mobile/screens/confirmation) and individual screen pages expose full-size screens via og:image and bytescale URLs; gallery labels sit one item ahead of the image they describe, so every file here was identified by reading it. Dribbble returned an empty 202 challenge body for every user agent, feed and JSON endpoint and an empty page through WebFetch, Behance returned 403, and Bing's image index surfaced no dribbble.com streak shots, so no Dribbble or Behance examples are included.
- A captioned overview of all 32 saved screens is at mobbin-dribbble/overview-sheet.png; per-app App Store contact sheets are under the appstore/ subfolder there.
- In the repo, the streak logic and card live at web/lib/contributions/streak.ts and web/components/home/contribution-streak.tsx (the card already implements 'longest streak' on quiet days and a text-only next marker); the celebration modal's strings were not found anywhere in the working tree, and the natural post-action homes for a streak line already exist in web/components/study/score-flourish.tsx (test hand-in) and web/components/study/my-record.tsx (own record with a privacy sentence).
- Every well-regarded streak moment fires as a step inside the completion flow (Duolingo's lesson-complete sequence, Finch, Streaks, Kindle), never as a modal on app open; MeltingPot is the outlier here and that is the root of the interruption complaint.
- The count is always paired with a seven-day (or month) row where done days are filled and covered days carry their own mark; no product draws an X, a red day, or a broken chain.
- Grace takes one of three shapes: pre-bought (Duolingo freeze), automatic and reported afterward (the snowflake), or self-declared in advance (Apple's Pause Rings, Streaks' task days); the self-declared, free form is the only one compatible with a product that refuses threats.
- The numbers that survive a break are lifetime totals, consistency rates and best runs (Streaks '297 completions, 80.1%', GitHub '3,285 contributions', NYT '537 puzzles'), and products that lead with those feel calm while products that lead with the live count feel tense.
- Big celebrations are reserved for milestones (7, 30, 100, 365) and their copy names the period ('a whole month', 'two weeks in a row') rather than reciting the count; ordinary days get a small tick-over.
- Copy verbs sort the good from the bad: 'grow', 'extend', 'keep', 'resume' in the gentle examples; 'lose', 'about to end', 'protect', 'days left' and mood-shifting mascots in the anxious ones.
- Several serious learning and practice apps (Waking Up, Khan Academy, Headspace, Brilliant) show no streak at all and lead with progress through material, which is the honest fallback if a streak cannot be made quiet enough.
- The research points one way: Silverman and Barasch (2023) show people treat a logged streak as a goal in itself, that a break blamed on oneself hurts most, and that a repair path softens the drop; Sharif and Shu (2017, 2021) show goals with a costly emergency reserve beat both easy and hard goals (55 percent versus 37 percent rebound after a missed day); Lally (2010) found a single missed day did not damage habit formation; Etkin (2016) found measurement can drain enjoyment; Fogg says the celebration must land immediately after the behaviour; Dai, Milkman and Riis show a new week or term is a natural restart.
- Duolingo's own published data undercuts the harsh version of streaks: permitted breaks (weekend amulet, two freezes) raised long-run activity, and their 2026 revival event concedes that losses 'beyond a user's control' demotivate people.

## What to refuse

- Loss countdown and paid rescue: Snapchat's chat row '325-day Streak ended · just now' with an hourglass and an orange Restore button, and the modal 'You can restore your Streak with Carlos for the next 24 hours. You have 1 free restore.' (snapchat-streak-restore-banner.png); MeltingPot must never show an ending, a timer, or a rescue.
- Monetized anxiety: Duolingo's Shop puts 'Streak Freeze, 5/5 EQUIPPED' above gem packs priced $4.99 to $19.99 (duolingo-blog-shop-streak-freeze-5-of-5-equipped.png); grace in MeltingPot should be a rule, never an item or a currency.
- Social broadcast baked into the celebration: Duolingo's 'Keep it up! I'm off to brag about your streak.' (duolingo-appstore-3-day-streak-celebration.png) and its 'Friends' and 'Leaderboards' notification rows; the private-only rule means no sentence may imply an audience.
- Painting failure: the Streaks widget marks a missed day with a bold 'x' (streaks-widgets-crop.png); a quiet day in MeltingPot is an empty dot at most.
- Leading with zero: Anki's Today panel headline is 'No cards have been studied today.' (anki-stats-today-calendar-heatmap.png); MeltingPot's card should lead with the run or the total, never with today's absence.
- Forced celebrations: Strava's help center says streak banners fire for weeks 2-5 and milestones and 'there is no feature available to disable these notifications'; MeltingPot's once-a-day modal on page load is the same mistake in a different place, and the fix is to move the moment to right after sharing or finishing a study run and to make it dismissable for good.
- Daily-reset pressure: Peloton's own blog confirms the daily streak resets on one missed day and Headspace's article admits people cry or get furious when the run streak drops to zero; keep the weekly or best-run figure standing so nothing ever reads as zero.
- Quota rings and targets: Peloton's '3/5 Active days, 5/7 Workouts' rings and Apple's ring-closing model turn a record into a goal to fail; MeltingPot counts what happened and sets no target.
- Comparison surfaces adjacent to the streak: Peloton's live leaderboard and Teams, Kahoot's podiums, Habitica's pets and gold, Strava's kudos counts; none of these belong near the contributions card.
- Confetti and mascots as the default: Finch's sunburst penguin with confetti and Duolingo's flame owl; MeltingPot's eight-dot confetti already pushes against the restrained-motion rule and should become a small settle on the number, honoring prefers-reduced-motion.
- Countdown against a goal: Halo Top Goal Getter's half-ring gauge from 0 to 66 with 'Day 1' and 'Only 65 more to go!' makes the first day look like nothing and turns the streak into a debt; MeltingPot's count must have no maximum.
- Prizes and social sharing: Halo Top's gift icons on calendar days ('the next prize you could win') and 'Share your stats on social with the hashtag' make a private record a public performance; keep 'Only you see this' and add no share button.
- Competition and bragging: Sugar's onboarding 'Share, compete and brag with your friends', its VS card (You 850kg vs Caitlin 970kg) and fire emoji are the exact comparison the owner forbids.
- Emoji rain as celebration: Sugar's 'Sweet, 17 clicks!' with heart-eyes and fire emoji falling is where confetti leads; MeltingPot's eight-dot confetti should go, not be tuned.
- Loyalty theatre: KFC Rewards' points, trophy on a pedestal, unlockable boxes, spinning 'EASY TO ENJOY' rings and 'WOOOW' type; nothing in MeltingPot should accrue points or unlock.
- Threat copy: the Streaks marketing site says 'Don't break the chain, or your streak will reset to zero days'; the app UI never says it, but the sentence is the kind of line that must not appear anywhere in MeltingPot.
- Pressure nudges with emoji: Tappsk's 'You're almost there' with a fire emoji under a 3/4 ring is a mild version of the same pressure; use a plain fraction or nothing.
- Timing: MeltingPot's current modal fires on page load; every restrained example ties the acknowledgement to the moment of the action (Gentler Streak's card after the workout, Streaks' ring closing on tap); move it to the share confirmation and the study-run summary.
- Interrupting the dashboard with a dialog is the pattern none of the good examples use; even Halo Top keeps its streak on the page rather than in a modal.
- Loss threats in copy: Paired's freeze card says 'applied when you lose your streak', Brilliant's charge says 'even if you miss a day', Kindle says 'Read tomorrow to grow your daily streak'; none of these sentences can appear in MeltingPot.
- Social comparison and bragging: Paired shows partner streaks side by side, Duolingo's owl is 'off to brag about your streak', Peloton's listing pairs the streak with a live leaderboard, and most screens end on a Share button (Drops, Streaks, Nike, Zero, Superhuman).
- Grey ladders of unearned milestones: Reddit's 5/10/20/30-day medallions with '2/5 days' bars, Zero's 'STREAKS 1/9', Atoms' fifteen hexagons, Aaptiv's greyed 'Daily Streak', Skillshare's '1 of 10' badges; MeltingPot should show one next marker in words and nothing unearned.
- Deficit framing: the hotel loyalty gauge '20 nights to go' and '10 more to Silver Elite', Brilliant's 'can you make it to 3 days in a row?'; never count down to a marker.
- Score keeping dressed as insight: Todoist Karma levels, points and 'Karma will decrease when tasks are overdue', Streaks' '80.1% all time' and charts, Peloton's '2 active days vs. previous month'; percentages, trends and levels are out.
- Zeros on screen: Superhuman's 'Marked Done, 0 conversations' row and AnkiMobile's 'No cards have been studied today'; a quiet day must show the run already managed.
- Persuasion statistics as pressure: Brilliant's 'People with a 3-day streak are 12x more likely to complete a course'.
- Takeover for a trivial state: Drops opens a full-screen purple modal for a one-day run over an empty month, and Superhuman's modal is mostly ones and a zero; MeltingPot's current day-one modal on Home load has the same problem and should become a line at the end of the share or study flow.
- Loud effects and off-brand surfaces: confetti on every card (Kindle, Yousician), glossy gold badges (Simply Piano), gradient tiles (Streaks), purple grounds (Drops, Simply Piano); keep the flat cream and orange system and reserve any motion for named markers under reduced-motion guards, as score-flourish.tsx already does.
- Imperative exclamations everywhere: 'Keep it up!', 'Don't let up!', 'BUILD UP YOUR STREAK', 'keep your daily streak', 'Strong start!'; MeltingPot's sentence-case, no-exclamation copy rule already excludes them, and 'Keep going' as a button label is the softest of the set but still an instruction, so a plain 'Close' or 'Back to the Pot' may fit better.
- A live zero beside a best (NYT Games 'Current Streak 0, Longest Streak 4'): show the longest run alone when the current run is zero.
- Selling protection or repair (Duolingo 'Refill streak freezes 400', '7-day Streak Shield 3000'; Snapchat 99-cent restores): never attach a cost to grace; MeltingPot has no currency and should not acquire one for this.
- Deadline glyphs and countdowns (Snapchat's hourglass four hours before expiry, Duolingo's '13 days left' on revival, the widget whose Duo grows desperate by evening, 11:50 pm last-chance pushes): no MeltingPot surface may change state as the day runs out.
- Betting on yourself (Duolingo's 'Wager 5 lingots' on a 7-day streak): pure loss aversion with a stake; do not build any 'commit to a week' device.
- A two-tier streak that shames grace use (Duolingo's Perfect Streak versus a streak that used freezes): one run, one meaning; a covered or paused day is not a lesser day.
- Social comparison in any form (Duolingo Friends tab and Friend Streaks, 'I'm off to brag about your streak', Snapchat mutual streaks, Finch goal buddies): the record stays private and MeltingPot's card must not grow a share button.
- Penalty mechanics (Habitica's avatar losing health for missed dailies): nothing in the Pot should be damaged, dimmed or greyed because a day was quiet.
- Firing the celebration on page load (MeltingPot's own streak-celebration.png shows the modal over Home while a correction is waiting): the Duolingo frames show the correct placement, immediately after the qualifying action, as a step with a Continue button.
- Streak creep, where the number becomes the point (The Decision Lab on a 566-day Duolingo streak kept through guilt; Particle: 'A person opening Duolingo for 30 seconds to preserve a streak is not learning'): keep the qualifying action real (a shared note, a completed run or test) so the record cannot be fed by a hollow tap.
- Guilt copy and mascot guilt (Duolingo's 'You made Duo sad' notifications and 'Use Duolingo today to make it 159' emails): no mascot opinion, no numbered nudge; MeltingPot's line 'Shared or studied today. Only you see this.' is the right register.
- Retroactive surprises that read as judgement: the Mobicip and lingoly images turned out to be stock illustrations or clickbait thumbnails rather than real screens and were not used; every example above is a real product screen that was viewed.

## The current modal, measured

- RIGHT: The card is placed last in the Home aside and streams on its own (web/app/home/page.tsx, Suspense), so it never delays the review queue; the numeral is Fraunces; there is no flame, no share button, no comparison, no count-down; the marker is spelled in words ('Five notes shared', 'Next marker at 10 notes.'); a quiet day already swaps the label to 'Longest streak' instead of printing a zero (contribution-streak.tsx); 'Only you see this.' and 'That is the longest run you have put together.' are the right register. Keep all of that.
- WRONG, timing (the root fault): the modal opens on Home load, not after the action. In streak-celebration.png it sits over 'Waiting on your review' and '1 correction is waiting on you' and covers the Review button; the person came to do a review and is told about a streak instead. None of the 21 reference screens I read fires on app open: Duolingo's tick-over is a stage of the lesson-complete sequence with Continue (duolingo-dof-post-lesson-streak-frames.png), Brilliant's is post-lesson on a calm white page (mobbin-web-brilliant-1-day-streak-interstitial.png), Gentler Streak's is a toast-sized card at the end of the workout on the device it happened on (gentler-streak-appstore-5.png), Superhuman's is triggered by reaching Inbox Zero itself. A once-a-day gate does not fix this; it only guarantees the interruption lands on whichever visit happens first, which is usually a visit for something else.
- WRONG, container: a centred dialog with an X and a full-width saturated primary button implies a decision, and there is none. 'Keep going' does not go anywhere; it dismisses. The dashboard's real primary action (Review, a small text link) is now visually outranked by an acknowledgement button. Every restrained example is inline (Peloton's one-row header, peloton-home-3-days-streak-week-strip.png; Gentler Streak's first card), and the only modal examples in the set are the ones the researchers flagged as heavy for the state shown (Drops' full-screen purple modal for a one-day run).
- WRONG, motion: the frame sheets (streak-research/lead/current-modal-entrance-crop-sheet.png, current-modal-confetti-crop-sheet.png) show the number climbing from 0 to 1, so a visible '0 day running' is on screen for a frame, which is the zero the owner's rule forbids; then an eight-dot ring bursts around the sprout circle. The honest tick is yesterday's count to today's (83 to 84 in the Duolingo frames), never from zero. The product already has one spark burst (ScoreFlourish on test hand-in), so a second burst with a different meaning cheapens both. A ring of dots around a circle icon is also the exact shape of Zero's badge and Yousician's confetti, both louder than the 'restrained motion' rule allows.
- WRONG, data truth on one screen: the modal says '1 day running' and 'Shared or studied today', while the card three hundred pixels away says 'Longest streak 1 day', which the component only renders when current is 0 (the run is not alive). Omair's last share was three days ago; the modal counted a study run, the card counts contributions only (its query reads contributions with status shared and never study_attempts). The rule says a day counts for a share OR a study; the card implements half the rule and the modal the whole rule, and they contradict each other in the same viewport.
- WRONG, the week strip: seven unlabeled dots read as a pager or carousel indicator (identical to the pager dots on the Streaks share card), not as days. With no weekday initials the fourth lit dot is unexplainable; Peloton, Duolingo, Finch, Babbel and Done all label the strip and give today a third state (outlined). The strip also cuts days in UTC (the card's own comment admits this), so for a Pacific student studying at 6 pm the wrong dot lights; a labelled strip would make that error visible.
- WRONG, copy: 'day running' is ambiguous (running is also jogging, and 'days running' is a journalist's phrase); the card says 'streak', the modal says 'running' and 'run', and the marketing card on /contributions says 'contribution streak' beside a Flame icon, so the feature has three names and two icons. 'Shared or studied today' is vaguer than the action just taken and pairs oddly with the card label. 'Keep going' is an instruction; it is the softest in the set but it is still Duolingo's verb family, and it appears at a moment when the person has already stopped. Kindle's 'Days in a row' and Zero's dated 'First fast, 24 Mar 2019' are the calmer nouns to borrow.
- WRONG, scale for the state: a takeover, a giant numeral and a burst for day 1 is the Drops mistake; Finch reserves the big moment for a month (finch-appstore-07.jpg) and Duolingo gives ordinary days a small tick-over. A first day deserves one sentence, not a ceremony, and the ceremony cheapens the seventh and thirtieth days when they come.
- WRONG, what it implies: a modal that appears unbidden once a day is a scheduled nudge, which is the mechanism behind streak anxiety even when the words are kind; the Duolingo widget and Strava banners work the same way and are the reason those products are criticised. The X plus a big button also implies there is something to accept. The owner's rules forbid threats; the calmest way to honour that is to never make the record appear on its own.
- WRONG, icon and surface: the sprout in a muddy tinted circle is a generic growth glyph unrelated to the brand mark; the modal exists only in dark (every capture is charcoal) while light is the shipped default (memory/decisions/020), so the moment has not been designed on cream paper at all.
- GAP, data model: the card caps at 400 rows and reads only shares, so the run and the strip cannot include study; study_attempts (record_flashcard_run, submit_practice_test) already exist and are per user, so the union is a two-line change in the Home query and one RPC or server action for the client surfaces. Until that lands, no surface can honestly say 'Shared or studied today.'
- GAP, persistence of the once-a-day gate is undocumented; if it is localStorage the modal fires again on every device and after every cleared session, which multiplies the interruption.

## Direction 1: Counted where it happened

**Concept.** Move the entire moment to the three screens that already mark the end of a qualifying action, and give it no container of its own: it is one row inside the success state, with no button, because those screens already carry the next actions (View in class notes, Back to class feed, Study the 3 still learning, Look back through the deck). The Home card becomes the ambient record in Peloton's one-row form: count, unit, labelled week strip. Nothing ever opens on its own, and the animation is the honest tick from yesterday's count to today's, played once, on the page where the person just did the thing.

**Anatomy.** SHARE SUCCESS (contribute-flow.tsx, step 'shared', under the existing 'Shared with the class' Settle and above the note card): a new RecordRow component. Left: Eyebrow 'Your record', then in Inter 14px 'Day 6 in a row. Only you see this.' Right: seven 20px dots with weekday initials above them (M T W T F S S, week starting Monday), past counted days filled brand orange, today filled brand orange with a 1px ink ring, uncounted days a hairline circle in edge colour, no future days drawn differently from past uncounted days (Kindle draws only what happened). First ever day: 'Today is on your record. Only you see this.' A day after a gap: 'Today is on your record. Your longest run is 6 days.' A second action on the same day: static row, 'Already on your record for today.' FLASHCARDS (flashcard-session.tsx Results, under 'X% of this round, this time through.'): the same RecordRow. PRACTICE (practice-session.tsx PracticeResults, appended to the 'Recorded as your first pass on this test.' line as a second sentence, then the strip): 'Recorded as your first pass on this test. Day 6 in a row, only you see this.' and the strip beneath the dl. HOME CARD (contribution-streak.tsx): replace the two StatCells with one row: Fraunces numeral '6' with 'days in a row' beside it, the labelled strip on the right; below it two small cells 'Notes shared 5' and 'Longest run 6 days' (the second only once longest is greater than 1); the marker line stays as it is. Rename every surface to 'days in a row' and 'longest run'; drop 'streak' from UI copy and swap the Flame on /contributions for the Plant icon. DATA: one RPC my_record(p_today date) or a server action that unions contributions(status shared, author_id) with study_attempts(user_id) dates, runs contributionStreak from streak.ts with the client's local date, and returns current, longest, active_today, last7 (seven booleans ending today) and counted_now (true only on the first qualifying action of that day). The client calls it after share_contribution resolves (line 508), after record_flashcard_run fires (study-workspace.tsx line 450), and after submit_practice_test resolves (line 408). The Home card query gains the study_attempts union so both surfaces agree.

**The moment.** Fires only when the success state mounts with counted_now true. Sequence, all under useReducedMotion like Settle and ScoreFlourish: the numeral in the sentence rolls from yesterday's count to today's with framer animate over 400 ms using the ScoreFlourish easing (5 to 6, never from 0; on a first day the sentence simply settles in with Settle); 150 ms later today's dot scales from 0.6 to 1 with the Settle curve over 240 ms and its ink ring fades in. No sparks, no burst, no sound; the ScoreFlourish sparks stay exclusive to the test hand-in. It does not leave: it is part of the page and stays until the person takes one of the page's own actions. On revisit or on the second action of the day it renders statically. Reduced motion renders the final state at once.

**A quiet stretch.** The row never shows a zero and never draws an X. After a gap, the first counted day says 'Today is on your record. Your longest run is 6 days.' and the strip shows the empty days as hairline circles among filled ones. On Home during a quiet stretch the row reads 'Longest run' with the Fraunces numeral of the best run and no current count at all (the label swap the card already does), the strip shows whatever happened in the last seven days with today as a hairline circle, and if nothing happened in seven days the strip is omitted rather than shown empty. No sentence about what is missing, ever; nothing changes as the day runs out.

**Markers.** Day markers at 7 and 30 only, spelled as periods the way Finch does: on the completion surface the sentence becomes 'A whole week in a row. Only you see this.' or 'A whole month in a row. Only you see this.', set in Fraunces 22px instead of Inter 14px, settling in with Settle; no confetti, no badge. ## Direction 2: The quiet card

**Concept.** Delete the celebration entirely and let the Home card be the whole feature, the way Streaks, Peloton, GitHub and Apple carry a record with no moment at all. The number lives as an ambient figure the person reads in passing; the only acknowledgement at the point of action is four extra words on a line that already exists. This is the least code, the least risk against the owner's rules, and the honest fallback if the deadline squeezes: nothing appears unbidden because nothing appears at all.

**Anatomy.** HOME CARD (contribution-streak.tsx, same slot): header 'Your contributions' with the Plant icon; a first row in Peloton's form: Fraunces 28px numeral, 'days in a row' in Inter 13px muted beside it, and on the right a labelled seven-day strip (M T W T F S S, filled orange for counted days, today with an ink ring, hairline circles otherwise); a second row of two small cells 'Notes shared 5' and 'Longest run 6 days'; the marker block unchanged; the footer line becomes 'Today is on your record. The card query unions study_attempts so studying counts, and it accepts the reader's local date from a cookie set by the theme script or falls back to UTC. COMPLETION SURFACES: no new element; the practice results line gains a clause, 'Recorded as your first pass on this test, and today is on your record.'; the flashcard Results subline gains a sentence, 'Today is on your record.'; the share success subline becomes 'Your contribution is live and credited to you. Today is on your record.' Each only when counted_now is true (the same RPC as Direction 1, but only its boolean is used). No strip, no numeral, no button anywhere but Home. CONTRIBUTIONS PAGE (/me/contributions, Shared tab header): optionally the same one-row record above the list, since that is the page the sidebar's 'Contributions' link opens.

**The moment.** None. The moment is ambient: the next time the person opens Home, today's dot is filled and the footer sentence is present. **A quiet stretch.** The first row swaps to 'Longest run' with the best count and no current figure (the existing label swap), the strip shows the last seven days honestly with today as a hairline ring, and the strip is dropped when there is nothing in seven days so the card never presents a row of empty circles. ## Direction 3: Weeks in a row

**Concept.** Keep the owner's rule that a day counts when the person shares or studies, but make the durable run a run of weeks: a week is on the record when any day in it counted. This matches how a course actually runs (a Tuesday lecture, a weekend of revision) and makes grace structural instead of mechanical: a quiet Wednesday never touches anything, so there is no freeze, no repair, no pause to design. Peloton keeps a weekly streak beside the daily one for exactly this reason, Strava's whole streak is weekly, Apple's Perfect Week and Kindle's weekly streak are the same idea. The daily surfaces still acknowledge the day; the number that grows is weeks.

**Anatomy.** LOGIC (streak.ts): add weekStreak(dates, today, weekStartsMonday) returning currentWeeks, longestWeeks, activeThisWeek, firstDayThisWeek; keep contributionStreak for the day strip and tests. HOME CARD: first row, Fraunces numeral '3' with 'weeks in a row' beside it; on the right the current week as a labelled seven-day strip (same drawing rules as Direction 1); under it a row of the last eight weeks as small rounded pills, filled orange for weeks on the record, hairline for weeks that were not, the current week outlined (this is a single row of eight cells, not a heatmap, so it stays clear of the SPEC's excluded contribution graph); second row cells 'Notes shared 5' and 'Longest run 5 weeks'; marker block unchanged; footer 'Today is on your record. Only you see this.' when active today. COMPLETION SURFACES (the same three insertion points as Direction 1): on the first counted day of a week the RecordRow reads 'This week is on your record. Three weeks in a row, and only you see this.' with the Fraunces numeral rolling; on any later day of the week it reads statically 'Tuesday is on your record.' with the week strip and today's dot filled. Avoid 'in the pot' phrasing: Pot is the product noun for a class space and the pun collides with it. DATA: the same my_record RPC returns the week fields as well.

**The moment.** Fires once per week, on the completion surface where the first counted day of that week happened: the weeks numeral rolls from last week's count to this week's over 400 ms with the ScoreFlourish easing, the current week pill in the eight-week row fills with the Settle curve, and today's dot fills 150 ms later. Every other day of the week gets the static row and the dot fill only. Nothing opens; nothing bursts; reduced motion renders the final state. It leaves with the page like any other line on it.

**A quiet stretch.** A quiet day is invisible by design; the weekly figure does not move. After a quiet week the first row swaps to 'Longest run 5 weeks' (no current figure), the eight-week row shows the honest gap as hairline pills with no X and no colour change, and the current week's strip shows today as a hairline ring. On the next counted day the completion row says 'This week is on your record. Your longest run is 5 weeks.' No zero appears anywhere and nothing changes state as the week runs out (no hourglass, no 'ends Sunday').

**Markers.** Week markers instead of day markers: 4 weeks ('A month of weeks in a row'), 12 weeks ('A whole term in a row'), spelled in Fraunces on the completion row with a Settle and no confetti. Note-count markers unchanged. At 12 weeks the Home card adds a dated line 'A term in a row, 6 Dec' that stays permanently. No badges, no grey ladder of future markers.

## Recommendation

Build Direction 1, 'Counted where it happened'. It fixes the one fault every other problem hangs off (the moment fires on page load) by moving it to the three screens that already end a qualifying action, and those screens already own their next buttons, so the acknowledgement needs no container, no X, no 'Keep going' and no once-a-day gate or storage. It keeps a single number and a single vocabulary ('days in a row', 'longest run'), the honest tick from yesterday's count to today's (never from zero), one labelled strip drawn the same way on Home and at the point of action, and a quiet stretch that reads as the run already managed. It is buildable in a few hours from what exists: Settle, Card, CardSection, Eyebrow, the framer animate pattern already in ScoreFlourish, contributionStreak in streak.ts, and three known insertion points (contribute-flow.tsx after share_contribution at line 508, flashcard-session.tsx Results, practice-session.tsx PracticeResults), plus one RPC or server action that unions contributions with study_attempts and accepts the client's local date, which also repairs the contradiction visible today between the modal's 'Shared or studied today' and the card's 'Longest streak'. Do the Home card and data union first (that half is Direction 2 and is the safe stopping point if hours run out), then the three rows, then the 7 and 30 day sentences. Direction 3 is the better long-term rule for a class product and should be recorded as a decision to revisit with the owner, but it changes the counting unit the owner specified in days and is not a fix to ship under deadline pressure. Delete the modal and its eight-dot burst outright rather than tuning them; the ScoreFlourish sparks remain the product's only burst, reserved for handing in a test.

- Done habit tracker (App Store listing, Today list) (appstore-done-today-list.png): Goal fractions per habit are targets; MeltingPot has none and should not invent a daily quota. Only the ticked completed row (done, no number) is a quiet form.
- Streaks (iOS, onboarding) (mobbin-streaks-ios-screen.png): Low relevance beyond the deliberate hold gesture for marking a day; MeltingPot's days are counted from real actions (share, study) so no manual marking is needed.
- Duolingo (post-lesson streak increment, current app) (duolingo-dof-post-lesson-streak-frames.png): Move MeltingPot's celebration to the two completion surfaces (the confirmation after 'Share with class' and the results screen of a flashcard run or practice test) and make it a step in that flow with a Continue button, never a modal on Home load. The visible tick from yesterday's number to today's is the honest form of the moment: it only makes sense right after the action, which is exactly the fix for the current page-load weakness. Keep the seven-day row; drop the 'can you keep it next week' question, which is a soft threat.
- Duolingo (streak calendar with used freezes) (duolingo-dof-streak-calendar-freeze-tracker.png): Take the calendar grammar: filled days for done, a distinct neutral mark for a covered day, and no X ever drawn. MeltingPot can render a quiet stretch as plain unfilled dots inside the same row rather than as a break. Do not take the two-tier 'Perfect Streak' that makes a freeze feel like a lesser achievement, and do not take the shop; grace in MeltingPot should be free, automatic and unnamed.
- Duolingo (streak freeze upsell) (duolingo-streak-freeze-equipped-blog.png): This is the pattern to name in a decision note and refuse: manufacturing a threat, then selling relief from it. MeltingPot has no currency and must never gate protection. The evidence Duolingo cites does support giving people slack, so keep the slack (a covered day or a pause) and drop the transaction and the interruption.
- Duolingo (streak wager, weekend amulet, reminder email, 2020) (duolingo-blog2020-streaks_wager1.png): The one transferable finding is Duolingo's admission that permitted breaks increase long-run activity. Build the break into the rule instead of selling it: for a class product, weekends and declared holidays should simply not count against a run. The wager is a bet against yourself and the email is a numbered nudge; both violate the owner's no-threat rule and neither belongs in MeltingPot, which has no reminder channel and should keep it that way.
- Duolingo (Streak Revival event, June 2026) (duolingo-streak-revival-event-androidauthority.jpg): Silverman and Barasch found that a repair option attenuates the drop in engagement after a break, and that breaks people attribute to themselves hurt most. MeltingPot can honour this without a countdown: the longest run is always shown as the record the person already made, and any share or study simply starts a new run beside it. Earning something back through the real activity is fine; a '13 days left' deadline is a loss threat and should not be copied.
- Duolingo (home-screen widget and long-streak page) (duolingo-dof-widget-rotator.png): The mood-shifting widget is the clearest loss-aversion device in the market and the thing critics mean by streak anxiety; MeltingPot should have no ambient surface that changes tone with the hours. The Friends tab is comparison and is out under the owner's rules. The only reusable bit is the verb 'extend', which is additive, versus 'lose', which is not.
- Duolingo (App Store listing, 'keep your daily streak') (duolingo-appstore-06.jpg): This is structurally the closest sibling of MeltingPot's current modal (icon, big number, unit, seven dots). What differs is intent: 'brag about your streak' makes it a social object, and MeltingPot's is private by rule. Keep the number-plus-week-row shape, but pair it with a sentence about what was done today rather than a mascot's opinion, and keep the 'only you see this' line.
- Finch (30-day streak celebration) (finch-appstore-07.jpg): Two things to take: the big moment is reserved for a milestone (30) rather than every day, and the copy names the period ('a whole month') instead of reciting the count. The repaired day is drawn inline with its own mark, not hidden and not a break. MeltingPot's daily moment can be small and in-flow; the milestone moment (7, 30) can be the one with the eight-dot confetti.
- Gentler Streak (streak as a band, no number) (gentler-streak-apple-article-3.jpg): A streak can be a range you stay inside rather than a chain that snaps. For MeltingPot's private record, consider a 30-day band or sparkline of share and study days with one plain sentence, and treat a quiet stretch as 'still within your usual rhythm' rather than as a fail. The exact day count can live one tap deeper, where only the person goes looking for it.
- Apple Fitness (Pause Rings, watchOS 11 and iOS 18) (apple-fitness-pause-rings-iphone.png): The best-behaved grace mechanism found: chosen in advance, free, in plain language, with a visible resume date and a neutral rendering. MeltingPot should offer 'Pause your streak' in settings for exam weeks and holidays, render paused days as neutral dots, and borrow the subtitle pattern ('while keeping your run').
- Kindle (Reading Insights daily streak) (kindle-appstore-shot-2.jpg): The verb 'grow' and the forward-looking 'tomorrow' are approach framing rather than loss framing, and the calendar shows only what was done. Kindle's parallel weekly streak is a gentler unit worth considering for a class product: 'weeks in a row with at least one share or study' matches the rhythm of a course and survives a busy Tuesday.
- NYT Games (Statistics screen) (nytgames-appstore-05.jpg): A live zero beside a best is precisely what the owner forbade, and this screen shows how flat it feels. MeltingPot's card should show 'Longest run' alone whenever the current run is zero, and the durable headline should be a lifetime total (notes shared, study sessions completed), the way '537 puzzles solved' carries this screen.
- Quizlet (achievements with dated streak badge) (quizlet-appstore-06.jpg): A dated marker is a record that cannot be lost, which fits the owner's rule that a quiet stretch shows what was already managed. MeltingPot already has 'Next marker at 10 notes'; extend that into a short list of dated markers ('First 7-day run, 12 Aug') instead of surfacing a live counter on Home. Quizlet's badge-as-trophy styling is louder than MeltingPot's language; keep the idea, not the medal.
- Streaks (all-time statistics and task days) (streaks-appstore-02.jpg): When a chain resets, a consistency rate and a completion count still tell the truth about the person; MeltingPot's private record can show 'days active in the last 12 weeks' and total sessions alongside the best run. Letting the person declare which days count (no weekends, or only class days) is a structural form of grace that needs no freeze.
- GitHub (contribution graph after streak counters were removed, May 2016) (github-2016-profile-after-streak-removal.png): A heat map of days plus a lifetime total is a complete private record with no threat in it. MeltingPot's Contributions page could carry a private 12-week heat map of share and study days and a total, and the decision note can cite GitHub as a major product that removed public streak counters specifically because they pushed people to work without rest.
- Snapchat (Snapstreak with hourglass, and paid Streak Restore) (snapchat-hourglass-chat-list-beebom.jpg): Everything here is on MeltingPot's forbidden list: a mutual obligation, a countdown glyph in a list view, and a paid restore. The specific warning is the hourglass: any icon that changes state as a deadline approaches is a threat even when no words are used. MeltingPot's dots must never change meaning as the day runs out.
- Habitica, Waking Up, Khan Academy, Headspace (contrast set) (habitica-appstore-01.jpg): Serious learning and practice products routinely ship with no streak at all and lead with progress through material; that is the credible alternative to keep in mind if the owner ever decides the streak is not earning its place. Habitica's damage mechanic is the penalty pattern to avoid. MeltingPot's equivalents of 'Day 4 of 28' already exist (notes shared, notes covered in a Pot, study sessions) and can carry the Home card on their own.
